## 🧪 Practical Lab Overview (IBM Cognos Analytics)

* **Practical 1: Transactional Sales Logging & Base Pricing**
  * **Objective:** Audit granular, chronological order transactions across timeline logs.
  * **Key Focus:** Date-level transactional tracking, unit price evaluations, and quantity monitoring.

* **Practical 2: Hierarchical Grouped Sales Reporting**
  * **Objective:** Structure multi-level grouped summaries with subtotals across nested hierarchies (`Region` → `Store Name` → `Product Category`).
  * **Key Focus:** Tracking sales amounts and line-item distributions across East, South, and West territories.

* **Practical 3: Volume vs. Revenue Distribution**
  * **Objective:** Compare category-level unit volume movement against generated gross sales.
  * **Key Focus:** Side-by-side graphical evaluations for core retail categories (`Furniture`, `Clothing`, `Grocery`, `Electronics`).

* **Practical 4: Multi-Metric Matrix Cross-Tabulation**
  * **Objective:** Build a dimensional cross-tabulation matrix measuring regional performance against product lines.
  * **Key Focus:** Cross-evaluation across 4 geographic regions yielding **₹7,84,475.00** total sales volume across **755 total units**:
    * **Electronics:** ₹2,60,822.00 *(Highest revenue segment)*
    * **Furniture:** ₹1,77,328.00
    * **Grocery:** ₹1,75,273.00
    * **Clothing:** ₹1,71,052.00
    * **Top Region by Volume:** South Region (285 units sold; ₹1,16,630.00 in Electronics alone).

* **Practical 5: Sliced Dimensional Subtotals & Filtering**
  * **Objective:** Apply slice-and-dice mechanisms to isolate specific regional-product intersections.
  * **Key Focus:** Filtered rollup matrices isolating sub-group revenue totals and volume counts.

* **Practical 6: Interactive Executive BI Dashboard**
  * **Objective:** Develop an operational business intelligence dashboard in **IBM Cognos Analytics**.
  * **Key Visualizations:**
    * **Summary KPI Card:** High-level Gross Sales Metric (**81.3K** Sales Amount).
    * **Time-Series Area/Line Chart:** Temporal sales performance trends across key sales dates.
    * **Category Horizontal Bar Chart:** Sales Amount distribution across product categories led by Electronics and Furniture.
